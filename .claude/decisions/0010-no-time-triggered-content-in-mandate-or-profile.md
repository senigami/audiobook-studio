# OD-0010: Nothing time-triggered goes in the mandate or a profile; session state goes in the handoff
Status: accepted        Date: 2026-07-29
Scope: CLAUDE.md orchestrator section; every seat profile
Context: Items like "pending the owner's generated image" or "rebase once PR #A merges" become permanent noise the day they resolve, because nobody revisits a mandate to delete a bullet, whereas `.memory/HANDOFF.md` is rewritten each checkpoint and pruned. This is a new convention adopted during the 2026-07-29 conventions upgrade; no verbatim sentence stating it existed yet in CLAUDE.md or crew-doctrine.md at time of writing — flagged rather than invented, per instructions.
Decision: "**Nothing time-triggered goes in this section or in a profile** — "pending the owner's image", "rebase once PR #A merges", "currently unwired" are session state and belong in `.memory/HANDOFF.md`, which something actually prunes; parked here they become permanent noise the day they resolve (OD-0010)." — CLAUDE.md, Orchestrator identity & mandate (Roster paragraph).
Consequences: Forbids parking session state in an auto-loaded file. Blocks the specific reflex of recording a pending item where it will definitely be re-read, which is exactly what makes it permanent: nobody revisits a mandate to delete a resolved bullet, so the item becomes noise paid for on every dispatch. `.memory/HANDOFF.md` is the home because something actually prunes it.
Disconfirming evidence: A time-triggered item placed in the handoff instead of the mandate is lost or overlooked at the moment it resolves, causing real rework — i.e. HANDOFF.md's pruning discipline turns out to be less reliable than mandate residency for this class of content.
