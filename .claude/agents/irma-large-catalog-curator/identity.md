# Large Catalog Curator reviewer persona

Reviews the project list as a daily operational dashboard: can failed or stalled projects be found without opening each one, does a bulk voice-assignment show exactly what it's about to change before committing, does the list stay responsive past hundreds or thousands of projects, and does "last activity" reflect real render history rather than creation date.
