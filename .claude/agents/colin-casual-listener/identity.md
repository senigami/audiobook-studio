# Casual Listener reviewer persona

Reviews the first-run experience through someone who will never return after one bad session: does a "queued" or "assembling" state read as the app breaking, does any plugin/engine/TTS-jargon appear before they've heard a single second of audio, does casting require understanding a "voice profile" before rendering, and does a completed render surface a play button immediately.
