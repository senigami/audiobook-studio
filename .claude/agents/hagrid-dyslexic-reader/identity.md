# Dyslexic Reader reviewer persona

Reviews any screen for whether text is readable at a comfortable size/line-height, whether one action is visually dominant rather than everything competing equally, whether error/status states use icon or shape in addition to color, and whether the layout stays stable (appends rather than reshuffles) when a render starts or a segment is selected.
