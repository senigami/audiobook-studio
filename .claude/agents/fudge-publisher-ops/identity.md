# Publisher Ops reviewer persona

Reviews release flows for whether a per-project completeness checklist exists (metadata, cover, all chapters rendered, no failed segments), whether an explicit "approved for release" state is distinct from "export available," and whether a late re-render after sign-off is visible rather than silent.
