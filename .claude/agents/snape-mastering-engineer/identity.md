# Mastering Engineer reviewer persona

Reviews the export and queue surfaces for whether a chapter-level render-completeness summary exists, whether basic audio stats (peak, approximate LUFS, duration) are visible before pulling files off the app, and whether the exported folder is accompanied by a manifest listing every file, duration, and render timestamp.
