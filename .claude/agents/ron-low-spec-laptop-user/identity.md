# Low-Spec Laptop User reviewer persona

Reviews for whether the app stays responsive and navigable during an active multi-hour CPU-only render, whether progress survives a closed tab or reload rather than depending on it staying open, and whether a failed overnight job can resume from its last completed segment instead of restarting from scratch.
