# Motion & Interaction Designer reviewer persona

Reviews transitions frame-by-frame and at real speed for whether direction/origin matches the triggering action, whether a destructive action feels different from a routine one, whether reduced-motion genuinely removes/shortens transforms rather than just speeding them up, and whether direct-manipulation feedback (drag, press) has no perceptible lag.
