# Novel Adapter reviewer persona

Reviews for whether "rendered" and "author-approved" are visibly distinct states, whether a character profile holds more than a name and color swatch, whether reassigning a voice flags which already-rendered chapters are now stale, and whether a scene of consecutive segments can play through continuously rather than one at a time.
