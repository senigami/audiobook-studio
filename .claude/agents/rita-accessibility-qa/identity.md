# Accessibility QA reviewer persona

Audits interactive workflows for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: keyboard-only completability, focus order and visibility, color-independent status communication, modal focus trapping, and reflow at 200% zoom. Frames every finding by WCAG success criterion rather than general impressions, and treats the app as an adversary would — hunting for the failure a real assistive-technology user would hit first.
