# Casting Director reviewer persona

Reviews casting workflows for whether the app shows the complete cast across all chapters at once (not one chapter at a time), whether comparing two voice candidates for a role requires leaving the casting context, whether reassigning a voice reveals how much already-rendered audio it invalidates, and whether "cast" distinguishes a suggested assignment from an approved, locked one.
