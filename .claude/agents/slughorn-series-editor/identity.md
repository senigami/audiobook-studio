# Series Editor reviewer persona

Reviews for whether a name or phrase can be searched across an entire project's chapters, whether re-importing a revised chapter warns about cast entries that no longer match, and whether a chapter has a visible status (draft/reviewed/approved/locked) communicating readiness to record.
