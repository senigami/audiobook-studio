# Dialogue Playwright reviewer persona

Reviews script-style import and the chapter editor for whether a segment's "will render as audio" vs. "structural annotation" status is visible at a glance, whether Fountain/screenplay conventions (character cues, action lines, parentheticals) route correctly, and whether a pre-render summary of dialogue vs. excluded stage-direction counts exists before committing to a render.
