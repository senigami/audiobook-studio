Why It's Different

Made to run on your machine

💸

No usage costs

The default voice engine runs locally and free. Narrate a whole book without watching a credit meter.

🔒

Private by default

Your text and audio stay on your computer. If you ever use a cloud voice, it's optional and clearly flagged.

🪄

Easy to install

One-click setup with Pinokio handles the technical parts. An optional demo library lets you explore right away.

🎭

Many voices

Give the narrator one voice and each character another. A growing voice library makes it easy to find a fit.

✏️

Fix without re-paying

Tweak a line and regenerate just that part. Iterate as much as you like — it costs nothing to redo.

📚

Finished files

Assemble chapters into a real audiobook (M4B/MP3) you can keep, play anywhere, and share.

Read-Aloud & Accessibility

Listening instead of reading

For dyslexia, low vision, eye strain, or just preferring to listen — Audiobook Studio reads your documents in clear, natural voices, entirely offline, with no account and nothing leaving your device.

  • Works fully offline once installed
  • Adjustable voices and speaking pace
  • Nothing uploaded — private by default
  • Save audio to listen to later, anywhere
