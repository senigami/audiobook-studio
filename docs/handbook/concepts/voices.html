Voices, Variants & Samples

A voice in Studio is a small hierarchy of its own. Understanding it makes the Voice Lab straightforward.

At a glance
  • A Voice is a reusable identity.
  • A Variant is a tuned version of that voice for an engine.
  • Samples are the audio a variant is built from.
  • Voice identity is kept separate from engine assets.

Voice vs variant

A voice is the identity; a variant is a usable build of it. One voice can have several variants — for different engines, speeds, or styles.

Samples & rebuilds

Variants are built from sample audio. Add or replace samples and rebuild to improve a clone. Quality depends a lot on the samples — see Voice Cloning Quality.

Engine-per-voice & portability

A voice's identity (name, image, tags) is separate from its engine-specific assets. That's what lets a voice move between engines, and what makes voices portable as shareable bundles.

Coming with 2.0 — voices become shareable bundles with an icon, a playable sample, and rich tags, published to a Hugging Face library. See Voice Icons & Tags.
