The 2.0 Rearchitecture

Everything that makes 2.0 different

Studio 2.0 moves synthesis into a managed, crash-isolated TTS Server and opens up engines and voices as installable, shareable bundles.

🧩

Managed TTS Server & plugins

Engines run in a separate, crash-isolated process managed by Studio. An engine can fall over without taking your work down.

🔀

Engine registry & voice bridge

One clean boundary routes every synthesis request, so voices stay independent of any single engine.

⬇️

Install engines like Stable Diffusion

Browse a tag, clone it down, and it's installed. Engines are normal repos you can update with a click. XTTS is the local default; cloud engines are optional and opt-in.

Planned for 2.0
🎙️

A real voice library

Voices carry an icon, a playable sample, a description, and rich tags — browse, download, and share them like a proper library.

Planned for 2.0
🤖

AI voice casting

Describe a character and get matched to the right voice from your library, with a reason for every pick. You stay in control.

Planned for 2.0
🔒

Local-first & private

Your manuscript, samples, and output stay on your machine. Anything that leaves is explicit, opt-in, and clearly disclosed.

🔌

TTS gateway API

Use Studio as a local text-to-speech backend over HTTP for your own scripts and apps, through the engines you've installed.

📦

Plugin SDK

A small, published contract (five methods) lets anyone wrap a new engine and share it. Built-in engines use the exact same path.

♻️

Robust queue & recovery

Reliable progress, reuse of unchanged audio, and recovery after a restart — completion is decided by validated artifacts, not loose files.
